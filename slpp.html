Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 9, 2026

Welcome to this app ("the App"). We value your privacy and the protection of your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information, as well as your related rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Photo Library access data: after you grant permission, the App accesses video assets in your system Photos library for browsing, filtering, moving, and deleting actions.
  • Local file data: when you choose "Archive", videos are saved to the App's sandboxed local library for offline management.
  • Basic diagnostic information: iOS may provide limited crash or performance data to help maintain app stability.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core features: browse videos, select videos, move to local library, restore to system Photos, share, and delete.
  • To improve experience: optimize loading speed, stability, and interaction quality.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • By default, the App does not upload your videos to our servers.
  • Video files are primarily stored on your device (system Photos library or the App's local sandbox).
  • We take reasonable safeguards to help protect your data from unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal video content with third parties unless you explicitly consent or disclosure is required by law.

5. Your Rights

  • You can revoke Photos permission at any time in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete local library videos in the App, or restore videos to system Photos for further management.

6. Children

If you are a minor, please use the App under the guidance of a parent or guardian.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy based on feature changes or legal requirements. The latest version and effective date will be shown in the App.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at lin2k@live.com.
